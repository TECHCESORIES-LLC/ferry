AMS Connect Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

AMS Connect is the school app for Atlanta Marathi Shala (“AMS”), a volunteer-run Marathi language school. The app is built and operated by Techcesories LLC (“we”, “us”) for AMS. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Who uses the app

AMS Connect is for AMS students, parents and guardians, teachers, librarians and administrators. Accounts are set up or approved by AMS. Students under 13 use the app only through an account AMS sets up with their parent’s or guardian’s consent.

What we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, role (student, parent, teacher, librarian or administrator), class or grade, and which students are linked to which parent.
  • School records: homework assignments and submissions, including files students upload, grades, attendance, calendar events and library checkouts.
  • Content people post: announcements, events, and class photos uploaded by teachers or staff.
  • Notification data: a push notification token for your device, so we can send school alerts.
  • Basic technical data: app version, device type and error logs, used to fix problems.

AMS Connect shows no ads, does not ask for your location or contacts, and does not take card or bank payments.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the school through the app: sharing announcements, homework, grades, attendance, events, photos and library records with the right people, sending notifications, keeping accounts secure, and fixing bugs.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Who can see it

  • Inside the app, by role: students see their own work and grades. Parents see their linked children’s information. Teachers see their own classes. Librarians see library records. Administrators can see and manage all school records.
  • Service providers: the app’s data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) servers in the United States. We may also use Google Firebase for sign-in, file storage and notifications, and Apple Push Notification service to deliver notifications. These providers process data only to run the app for us.
  • When the law requires it: we may share information if required by law or to protect the safety of a student or other person.

Children’s privacy

Many AMS students are under 13. We follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We collect only what the school needs for its classes.
  • Children’s accounts are created by AMS with a parent’s or guardian’s consent, given at enrollment.
  • The app has no public profiles and no chat with people outside AMS.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to review, correct or delete their child’s information, or to stop further collection, by emailing contactus@techcesories.com.

Class photos are visible only to signed-in AMS members.

Device features

If you choose to add a school event to your device’s calendar, the app writes only that event. It does not read your other events.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login private and tell us at contactus@techcesories.com if you think your account was misused.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while the account is active and while AMS needs the school records. When an account is closed, we delete its personal information within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law.

To delete your account or your child’s account: email contactus@techcesories.com from the email on the account, or ask an AMS administrator.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and tell users in the app about any significant change.

Contact

Techcesories LLC, contactus@techcesories.com
