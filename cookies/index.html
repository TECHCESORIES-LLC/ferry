Ferry Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Publisher: Techcesories LLC (Georgia, USA) · Product: Ferry website

This page explains how the Ferry website uses cookies and similar browser storage. The short version is that we set no advertising or tracking cookies, which is why you will not see a cookie consent banner here.

Do we use cookies?

The Ferry website sets no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking cookies, and no analytics profiling cookies. We do not build a profile of you or follow you across other websites.

Analytics

We measure visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-first analytics tool that is cookieless. It counts page views in aggregate, sets no cookies, and does not track you across sites or store personal information about you.

What is stored in your browser

The public pages of this site do not store cookies for tracking. Our private operator dashboard uses a single session-only value to remember a login for the person running it, and it is never set for ordinary visitors. The Ferry browser extension stores data locally on your own device in order to work, which is separate from website cookies and is described in our Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Because we set no non-essential cookies, there is nothing to opt out of here. You can still clear this site's stored data at any time through your browser settings without affecting how the site works.

Changes

We will update this page and its "last updated" date if our practices change.
