Questions

Does Ferry send my conversations anywhere?

Not to us. Ferry converts chats in your browser. Its only server is a license check, which receives a random install ID, plus your email only if you use Restore Pro. A chat leaves your device only when you send it to Notion or GitHub with your own key.

Does it work with Gemini?

Yes, free. Click Allow Gemini in the Ferry popup once and approve access to gemini.google.com in Chrome. Ferry then exports the Gemini chat you have open, and Pro can export your whole Gemini history.

Which browsers and systems work?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc, on Windows, macOS and Linux, with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

Does it work in Firefox or Safari?

Not yet. Ferry runs in Chromium browsers: Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

Is Pro a subscription?

Only the yearly plan, which renews and can be cancelled any time. The 1, 3 and 6 month passes end on their own. Files you exported stay yours.

How do I get Pro?

Install Ferry for free, open the Ferry popup and pick a plan. Payment goes through Stripe and Pro unlocks right away.

What if I reinstall or switch computers?

Use Restore Pro in the Ferry popup and enter the email you paid with. Your plan comes back without paying again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Every purchase has a 30-day money-back guarantee. Email us and we refund it. See the terms.
