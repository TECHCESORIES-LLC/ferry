Questions about Ferry

Does Ferry send my conversations anywhere?

No. Ferry converts chats in your browser and never receives them. A chat leaves your device only when you send it to Obsidian, Notion or GitHub with your own credentials.

Which browsers and systems work?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc, on Windows, macOS and Linux. Ferry supports ChatGPT and Claude.

Is Pro a subscription?

Only the yearly plan, which renews and can be cancelled any time. The 1, 3 and 6 month passes end on their own. Files you exported stay yours.

Yes. Every purchase has a 30-day money-back guarantee. Email us and we refund it. See the terms.
